# VidzyAI

VidzyAI is a simple way to chat with YouTube videos using AI. Instead of watching an entire video or scrubbing through it to find something, you can just ask questions and get answers based on the video's content. 

## What Does It Do?

- **Chat with any YouTube video** - Open a YouTube video and ask it anything, just like you're talking to someone who watched it for you.
- **Get quick summaries** - Quickly get key points, takeaways, or explanations without sitting through the whole video.
- **Answers from the video itself** - The answers come directly from what was said in the video, not from general internet knowledge.
- **Saves your chat history** - Your previous conversations with a video are saved so you can pick up where you left off.
- **Works right from your browser** - Just click the extension while watching a YouTube video and start asking questions.

## How It Works 

1. You open a YouTube video and click the VidzyAI extension.
2. The app grabs the video's transcript.
3. It builds a smart way to search through that transcript using AI.
4. You ask a question, and it finds the relevant parts of the video to give you an accurate answer.
